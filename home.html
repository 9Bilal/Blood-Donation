Who We Are?

Blood Buddies is for public donation center with blood donation members in the changing health care system. Specialist blood donors and clinical supervision. Increasing communication with our members. High quality assessment, diagnosis and treatment. Examine critically to ensure alignment. The extra care of a multi-disciplinary team.Blood donation is a voluntary act where individuals donate a portion of their blood to be used for medical purposes. The donated blood is used to help patients who require blood transfusions due to medical conditions, surgeries, accidents, or other emergencies.




Our Objective
 To provide safe blood products at a minimum community share.
To give free-of-cost supportive treatment to β-thalassemia major patients, including safe blood transfusion, iron chelation, laboratory tests, medications, and consultation.
To introduce a new horizon of quality in blood banking by delivering safe blood per the international standards at a very low community share and encouraging people to donate blood.
To establish a state-of-the-art institute of Hematology to spread knowledge of Hematology and Transfusion Medicine across the community.
To improve the quality, effectiveness, and prognostic value of hematological disorders, namely Thalassemia, Hemophilia, etc.
To conduct research to improve the outcomes for Thalassemia patients.
To adopt preventive strategies like awareness campaigns and carrier detection of the Beta thalassemia gene to stop the propagation of β-thalassemia major in Pakistan.


Our Mission
 To be recognized as the most compassionate, ethical, accessible, and best
quality health care provider in the field of Hematology, Blood Banking, and
Transfusion Medicine that shall meet the needs and expectations of our
patients by fulfilling all National and International Standards.

Our Mission
 The mission of Muhammadi Welfare foundation is to provide safe blood
transfusion services and diagnostic facilities without discrimination of
cast and creed with the best quality at minimum community share and to
give free of cost treatment to Beta Thalassemia patients.
